## Independent false-negative and counterfactual review

In addition to **all 366 machine direct-candidate metadata rows**, inspected 13 non-direct rows per thesis: nine highest available frozen rating-count rows plus four deterministic randomly sampled rows (`seed = 20260916 + int(sha256(thesis_id)[:8],16)`). This is **169 thesis memberships / 142 unique Yandex IDs**, NOT a full review of all non-direct members. This deliberately samples the machine's discarded surface and exposes failures. Examples:

- `451870` «Quarantine Zone»: machine `adjacent_candidate` for B inspection, but description clearly advertises checking passes, temperatures, appearances and admitting/denying people. Direct **inspection verb**; full 3D movement/economy differs from cheap desk UI.
- `96441` «Gummy Blocks»: machine `noise_candidate` for B procedural grid, instructions explicitly say to place cubes to clear full rows/columns. Direct block-placement loop.
- `182665` «Wood Block Classic»: machine `adjacent_candidate`, instructions say place blocks in horizontal/vertical lines; direct block-clear family.
- `555319` «Блок Слайдер»: line clearing via **sliding**, a different gesture within procedural-grid economics; do not imply identical placement verb.
- `548744` and `371963`: known direct point checks excluded from both frozen search unions entirely. Exact-ID `get_games` point observations, if performed separately, must retain distinct provenance and do not acquire search membership.

Counterfactual stress tests: Poki Slice Master and I Am Hall Security prove engagement for their **full** games, not an extracted one-day mechanic; the analogous Yandex game does not prove an affordable clone can win. `562662` is evidence of genetic systems but also evidence that the visible competing package is nontrivial. Rating counts are source snapshots, not downloads, DAU, revenue, retention or longitudinal velocity. No observed cohort permits an honest contemporary growth estimate.

## Audit limitations and final research disposition

- The 366 rows were classified on **frozen textual metadata**; no claim of having played 366 games or independently validated their visuals/economies.
- A 169-membership stratified false-negative audit finds counterexamples but does NOT certify that the remaining non-direct rows are all irrelevant. A negative search cannot prove platform-wide whitespace.
- A C entry is a positive control for matching an *advertised atomic mechanic*, not proof of an exact cheap full product. Comparing siblings requires their own evidence.
- For game-system decisions and explicit BUILD/DEFER/REJECT triggers see the dated synthesis in `DanilaH/decisions`; these data do not reorder the live production queue or authorize work while development is paused.
- Both final local CSV tables and the stratified audit trace were checked for row-count/identity consistency, but **an official Reaper analyst-review finalization using these new judgments has not been run**; the earlier built-in PASS verifies the immutable unreviewed source/intelligence artifacts only. This is a bounded evidence-complete research decision, not a claim of a new reviewed-intelligence ZIP.
