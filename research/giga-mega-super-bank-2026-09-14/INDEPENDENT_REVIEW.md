# Independent review — Giga Mega Super Bank Expansion Sweep

Date: 2026-09-14

## Review question

Does the final ranking survive a second pass that prioritizes the portfolio's actual objective — disproportionate opportunity at very low **complete** production burden — rather than novelty or headline external download counts?

## Findings

### 1. PASS — no production-queue promotion is justified

The new evidence is useful for the reserve bank, but none of it is stronger than first-party shipping data or strong enough to interrupt current active production. The result correctly keeps the production queue unchanged.

### 2. PASS WITH CORRECTION — machine zero-direct counts are not whitespace evidence

The largest methodological risk in the first-pass output was false absence. Wood carving is the clearest counterexample: the semantic classifier returned zero direct candidates, while manual frozen-surface inspection found several direct Yandex products with thousands of ratings. Broad model assembly shows the same issue at smaller severity.

Therefore all whitespace claims in `RESULT.md` are explicitly bounded by the frozen query surface plus manual audit.

### 3. PASS — Rock / Mineral Specimen is a legitimate top new bet

It combines:

- large external proof for the atomic stone-processing interaction;
- an observed Yandex processing adjacent rather than a direct target execution;
- a weak but real Yandex mineral-collection fragment;
- cheap content multiplication through shape / color / inclusions / finish / label / tray;
- a strong before/after and collection payoff.

The unproven part is the *specimen-cabinet wrapper*, not the tactile core. Keeping it as `HIGH-SIGNAL TRANSFER`, rather than calling it validated Yandex demand, is correct.

### 4. PASS AS DARK HORSE — Darkroom ranks high because of burden/reward asymmetry, not proven demand

Darkroom does **not** have mobile-scale traction proof comparable with Paper Fold, Sneaker Art, Gem Stack, or TAP! DIG! MY MUSEUM. Its high placement is justified only under the portfolio's asymmetric objective:

- very cheap 2D implementation;
- natural anticipation timer;
- dramatic image reveal;
- cheap authored-content multiplication;
- strong current analog-photography cultural signal;
- very thin direct Yandex supply.

If ranking were based primarily on demonstrated game demand, Darkroom would fall below Sneaker, Fossil, Origami and Model Kit. It must remain labeled `DARK HORSE`, not `validated demand`.

### 5. PASS WITH PORTFOLIO PENALTY — Sneaker is stronger than the initial sweep interpretation

External evidence is direct and large (`Sneaker Art!` 10M+). The frozen Yandex surface did not confirm a true sneaker-customization core. Complete burden is low and CMF is high.

However, the portfolio already contains Headphones and Digicam customization reserves. Sneaker therefore should not automatically become the next project merely because its market proof is cleaner. The final `PORTFOLIO-OVERLAP CAUTION` is necessary.

### 6. PASS — Fossil demand proof is unusually clean, but burden is not ultra-low

`TAP! DIG! MY MUSEUM!` validates almost the exact discovery → assembly → display grammar at 5M+ downloads. The weakness is production economics: fossils, excavation states, museum presentation and progression create more authored content than Darkroom or a layered customization product.

It belongs in the strong bank but below the cheapest asymmetric bets.

### 7. PASS — Origami's 100M+ proof does not cancel its content-factory risk

The atomic fold/reveal mechanic is unquestionably proven. The problem is complete production cost: a traditional Paper Fold execution requires many bespoke fold orders and reveal images. A future version would need a reusable collectible system or strong procedural/content multiplication before graduation.

### 8. PASS — Model Kit should not be promoted despite excellent evidence

External assembly proof is huge and the real-world model-kit trend is strong, but Yandex already has broad construction/model assembly and the attractive `sprue → paint → decal` version requires bespoke parts, 3D states and presentation. Under this portfolio's burden constraint it is a good market thesis but a weaker asymmetric production thesis.

### 9. PASS — Restoration is a monitoring seed, not a discovered winner

`Второй шанс: Мастерская реставрации` is extremely relevant structurally but had zero ratings in the frozen observation. External restoration proof exists, yet the broad-object format can become a content factory. Re-observe later; do not transfer external traction onto the new Yandex listing.

### 10. PASS — young-outlier scan does not reveal a better cheap grammar

The five-page feed sample is useful but not a platform census. The strongest young listings mostly reinforce already-known Resell / Squishy / Roblox-like / destruction/case families. Novel camouflage and drill/collect fragments carry substantially higher 3D/map/AI or content burden.

Lifetime `ratingCount / age` values are normalization proxies only and must not be described as current velocity.

## Sensitivity check

If **demonstrated external demand** is weighted highest, the order becomes approximately:

`Origami / Sneaker / Fossil / Model Assembly / Rock → Darkroom`

If **complete production burden + reward asymmetry** is weighted highest, the order becomes approximately:

`Darkroom / Rock / Sneaker / Print Press → Fossil / Origami → Model Assembly`

The published TOP-5 is therefore best read as a balanced **candidate cluster**, not precise cardinal measurement. The robust conclusions are:

- Rock belongs in the top cluster under both views.
- Sneaker belongs in the top cluster but suffers portfolio overlap.
- Fossil has strong demand proof but medium burden.
- Darkroom is the best cheap dark horse, not the best demand-proven idea.
- Origami is extremely demand-proven but needs a content-burden solution.
- Model Kit is attractive research-wise but not asymmetric enough at present.

## Final independent verdict

**PASS.** The sweep found genuinely new evidence and new bank candidates, corrected machine false negatives, did not over-promote young-outlier proxies, and did not use research novelty as an excuse to reorder active production.

No further same-day query expansion is recommended.
