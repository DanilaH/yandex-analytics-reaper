# Production Assessment v1

## 1. Why this is separate from taxonomy

Production burden depends on our team, engine, reusable systems, assets, AI tooling, experience, and quality bar. The same market concept can have different costs for different teams.

Therefore production assessment is candidate-specific.

## 2. Dimensions

```text
dev_complexity: xs / s / m / l / xl
asset_burden: low / medium / high
content_burden: low / medium / high
backend_burden: none / low / high
balancing_burden: low / medium / high
liveops_burden: low / medium / high
qa_burden: low / medium / high
mobile_adaptation_burden: low / medium / high
ai_assisted_fit: strong / medium / weak
```

Also store estimated MVP dev-day range, estimated asset work, technical risks, and reusable systems available.

## 3. Current portfolio constraint

Initial strategy prefers concepts plausibly deliverable as a polished MVP in <=7 development days. This is a portfolio strategy constraint, not a market fact.

## 4. Calibration

After every game compare predicted vs actual dev days, asset burden, QA/rework, and unexpected complexity. Production assessment should become personalized to the real workflow.
