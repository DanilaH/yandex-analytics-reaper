# Giga Mega Super Bank Expansion Sweep — 2026-09-14

## Decision summary

This sweep intentionally searched a different space from the earlier Mega Bank Expansion. It emphasized mechanic/verb-first grammars, external mobile-to-Yandex transfers, low-burden anticipation/reveal loops, and an independent Yandex-native young-outlier feed sample.

**Final outcome:** the sweep adds several useful bank candidates but does **not** reorder the active production queue. The best new asymmetric candidates are **Rock / Mineral Polish → Specimen Cabinet**, **Darkroom / Film Development**, **Sneaker Art / Custom Sneaker**, **Fossil Dig → Museum**, and **Origami Fold / Collect**. Model-kit assembly is strongly validated but loses on complete production burden. Wood carving is not a new whitespace opportunity; direct supply already exists on Yandex.

This is a bounded market result, not a claim of platform-wide absence.

## False-negative audit

### Wood carving — direct supply exists

The lexical classifier reported zero direct candidates because the declared theme vocabulary was too narrow. Manual inspection of the frozen surface found direct Yandex executions:

- `179776` — `Токарный станок` — 8,171 ratings at observation;
- `193649` — `Wood Cutting` — 7,298 ratings;

External mobile proof is also mature (`Woodturning` and related carving titles). Therefore **wood carving is rejected as a new whitespace thesis**. It remains evidence that simple shape → carve → polish/paint loops can work.

Frozen Yandex examples include:

## Independent interpretation / stop rule

The strongest methodological finding is that `semantic direct_candidate = 0` must never be interpreted as Yandex absence. Wood carving and broad model assembly demonstrate the failure mode directly. The final decision therefore combines:

1. frozen search surface;
2. full review of every machine direct candidate;
3. manual false-negative audit of suspicious zero-direct theses;
4. external game-demand evidence;
5. complete production burden and portfolio overlap.

No same-day synonym expansion is justified after this sweep. New research on these theses should occur only on a material trigger:

- new longitudinal traction on a known Yandex seed;
- a new external breakout product;
- a production slot that requires choosing among the top bank;
- first-party learnings from our shipped games that materially change the burden/reward model.

**Portfolio action:** add the ranked candidates to the bank. Do not displace the current production queue solely from this research cycle.
